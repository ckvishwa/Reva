# Phase C physical acceptance — pending

All rows below are `NEEDS_HARDWARE` until a human observes the behavior on real devices and records the device, OS/build, source commit, date, result and artifact in `tests/evidence/ledger.csv`. API 36 emulator and JVM results are software evidence only. Use a verified debug APK from the final C3 commit; no release tag is authorized by this checklist.

1. **Talk and interruption.** On the primary physical Android phone, grant the microphone, ask REVA a local Today question, interrupt its audible TTS answer, then repeat start/stop five times. Verify no stale audio or avatar mouth movement after cancellation. Capture a short video and logcat with secrets/transcripts removed. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
2. **Permission denial and revocation.** Deny microphone on first request; confirm a safe visible fallback and no capture. Grant, use Talk, revoke in Android Settings, return to the app and confirm capture stops and a new attempt handles denial. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
3. **TTS, avatar and lifecycle.** Observe synchronized mouth movement during actual speaker playback, reduced-motion option, offline local WebView assets, app background/foreground, rotation/restart, and session cleanup. Confirm no avatar network request. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
4. **Phone to PC T1.** Pair with the owner PC and the grant shown on both devices. From the phone, open Calculator on the PC. Verify actual window presence and matching request ID/status; a verbal claim alone is insufficient. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
5. **Phone to PC T2.** From the phone, ask to close Spotify on the PC. Verify it remains open until the owner approves on the PC screen, then verify the process/window outcome. Deny on PC once and confirm the phone reports denial. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
6. **Native phone action.** Set an alarm by phone voice and verify the system alarm appears with the requested time. Exercise reminder, calendar and share-sheet confirmation surfaces, including a denied/cancelled case. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
7. **PC-off offline Finance.** Power off the paired PC, disable hosted processing, ask a supported local Today/Finance question, and verify the result against the phone's Rust Kernel data. Ask an unsupported open question and verify REVA states that advanced reasoning is unavailable. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
8. **Standalone hosted question.** With PC off, owner enters their own API key on the physical phone, reviews the provider/data disclosure, enables hosted processing and both domain consents, then asks an open question. Confirm visible Standalone mode and answer. Disable either domain consent and verify a follow-up stays local; remove the key afterward. This step requires the owner's key and consent, and may incur provider charges. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
9. **Mode transitions.** Observe PC outage/recovery, network disconnect, hosted timeout, consent revocation, cloud disable and app restart; confirm visible mode and no silent egress. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
10. **Two-tier 30-minute profile.** On a documented midrange phone and a documented high-end phone, run an active voice session for 30 minutes each while collecting `python tools/android/profile_voice_session.py --serial <physical-adb-serial> --tier <tier> --out <local-jsonl>`. Record model, Android version, APK SHA-256, ambient/charging state, start/end battery, memory PSS, CPU, battery temperature, thermal status and any throttling. Attach the two JSONL files and an operator observation sheet to ledger rows. The script refuses emulators and labels its output observations rather than PASS. `NEEDS_HARDWARE`.
